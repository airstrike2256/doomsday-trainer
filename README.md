Doom

A terminal-based trainer for mastering the Doomsday Algorithm.

Overview

Doom is a command-line training tool designed to help you learn and memorize the Doomsday Algorithm—the mental math technique that allows you to determine the day of the week for any date.

Whether you're a calendar enthusiast, a competitive mental calculator, or just someone who enjoys learning obscure superpowers, Doom provides drills, challenges, and progress tracking to help you develop instant weekday recall.

Features
Interactive training sessions
Random date challenges
Difficulty levels
Timed practice modes
Accuracy and speed statistics
Progress tracking
Step-by-step learning mode
Historical and future date support

What is the Doomsday Algorithm?

The Doomsday Algorithm, developed by John Horton Conway, is a mental calculation method for determining the day of the week for any given date.
